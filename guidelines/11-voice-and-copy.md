# 11 · Voice and on-canvas copy

Design and copy are inseparable in Zluri's work: the layouts only hold because the headlines are short and specific. These patterns are **derived from the copy on the designers' frames and the live site** — they are not an official messaging guide. When the user supplies copy, use it; use this file to shape, trim or draft placeholder copy and flag anything you wrote yourself.

## Voice in four traits

| Trait | Sounds like | Not like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Clear** | "See Identities. Understand Access. Control Risks." | "Unlock next-generation synergies across your identity fabric" |
| **Expert, plain-spoken** | "Dormant accounts, excessive privileges, and autonomous agents remain active until after impact." | jargon stacks, buzzwords without a noun |
| **Confident, not hypey** | "Find Every Identity Risk Before Attackers Do" | "The world's #1 revolutionary AI platform!!!" |
| **Human** | "because fun should never be least privileged." | stiff corporate boilerplate; forced memes |

## Headline patterns that recur

| Pattern | Examples from the library |
| --- | --- |
| **Problem as a truth** | "You Can't Secure What You Can't See" · "The Identity Surface has Expanded" · "Identity Risk Changes Faster Than Access Reviews" |
| **Three-beat** | "See Identities. Understand Access. Control Risks." · "Toxic access combinations. Detected. Blocked. Before your next audit." · "What we built. What we shipped. What we scaled." |
| **Outcome + context** (ads) | "**Save 100+ IT Hours** in Google Workspace" |
| **Introducing** (launch) | "Introducing **Segregation of Duties (SoD)** in Zluri" · "Meet Zluri's **Identity Security Posture Management**" |
| **Named gap / concept** | "The **Access Visibility** Gap" · "**Samsara** Cycle" (trapped "every quarter forever") |
| **Question CTA** | "Still Running **Access Reviews** Manually?" · "Ready for **similar results?**" |
| **Event** | "{Event name} **{year/city}**" · "Meet us at **AWS re:Invent**" · "Brazil, meet Zluri!" |
| **Before/after pairs** (carousel) | "Can't Automate / What you can't see" · "Challenges: / How Zluri's Group-Based Access Reviews Help:" · "The Trick / The Treat" |

The coloured keyword is always the **thing** (product, event, outcome, named concept) — never an adjective like "powerful".

## Supporting lines

- One sentence, ≤ 20 words, concrete: "Most tools flag identity risk. Zluri automatically fixes it."
- Prefer two short sentences to one long one.
- Numbers beat adjectives: "50% faster provisioning", "150+ hours saved", "360,000+ apps discovered".
- On events: just the facts in rows — date, time (with zone), venue.

## Mechanics

- **Sentence case** for body, UI and most headlines; Title Case is acceptable in web H2s and event titles (follow the nearest reference). Never ALL CAPS beyond eyebrows.
- Eyebrows are 1–4 words, caps: "THE CHALLENGE", "CAPABILITIES", "HOW IT WORKS", "USE CASES", "WHY ZLURI", "FOUNDATION".
- Curly quotes and apostrophes (’ “ ”). En dash for ranges (28 – 30 January), em dash sparingly.
- Numerals for all numbers on canvas. "+" suffix for floors (425+). "%" tight to the number.
- Dates: "23 March 2026" or "September 24-26, 2025" (match the audience's locale; be consistent within a piece). Times with zone: "12:00 - 13:30 GMT-7".
- Product names and acronyms exactly as in `01-brand-foundations.md`. "SaaS", "AI", "IT", "non-human identities (NHIs)", "Shadow IT", "Shadow AI".
- No exclamation marks except greetings and culture posts ("Welcome to the Zluri Family!").
- No emoji in brand assets.
- Legal marks where the source uses them: "The Forrester Wave™", "Gartner®".

## Placeholder policy

If the user has not supplied copy, draft it in this voice and **say that you did**. Never ship lorem ipsum. Never invent statistics, customer names, quotes, awards or dates — use clearly bracketed placeholders (`[stat]`, `[Customer]`) and list them for the user.
