Introduction
Bringing Our Identity 
to Life
Our brand is built on clarity, simplicity, and purpose. 
Whether you’re designing an interface, writing a line 
of copy, or building a customer presentation, these 
guidelines exist to keep our message consistent 
and our experience seamless.
This document brings together the core elements 
that shape our brand—our voice, our visuals, and 
how we show up in the world. It’s more than just a 
rulebook. It’s a shared language that helps us all 
create with intention, empathy, and confidence.

Light and Dark Versions
The Zluri logo is designed for flexibility across 
different backgrounds. On light surfaces, the black 
wordmark ensures strong visibility. On dark 
backgrounds, it switches to white to maintain legibility 
and contrast.
Use these versions consistently to keep the logo 
clear and accessible in any setting.

--- PAGE 8 ---
Monotone logo
In cases where the full-color logo isn’t suitable—like 
over complex backgrounds, gradients, or limited-color 
printing—a monotone version should be used.
This version simplifies the mark while maintaining 
brand recognition. Use it in white over dark or colorful 
backgrounds to ensure maximum legibility and 
consistency.
Download Zluri logo files

--- PAGE 9 ---
Clearspace
To maintain clarity and impact, the logo should always 
be surrounded by enough space.
The minimum clearspace is defined by the height of 
the circular mark. No text, images, or other elements 
should enter this area.
This ensures the logo stays readable and visually 
distinct, no matter where it’s used.

--- PAGE 15 ---
Secondary Brand Palette
The secondary palette adds range and flexibility to 
the core Zluri system. These colors can be used to 
support illustrations, charts, UI elements, or moments 
where more energy or contrast is needed.
Use them thoughtfully and sparingly—primary colors 
should remain the foundation of most designs. The 
secondary palette is best suited for highlights, accent 
treatments, or extended brand moments.

--- PAGE 18 ---
Headings and Titles
Our headings are more than just text—they lead the story. They command 
attention without shouting, and guide the reader through every experience 
with clarity and confidence.
We use Satoshi for all headlines and display text. It pairs a clean geometric 
structure with just enough personality to stand out. It's digital-first, sharp in 
tone, and neutral enough to support any message—from visionary to practical.
Satoshi
Usage Guidelines
Use Satoshi Bold or Medium for primary headings.
Sentence case is preferred—avoid all caps unless intentional and minimal.
Maintain generous spacing—our headlines breathe best with whitespace.
Color choices should balance contrast and accessibility. Avoid placing heading 
text on cluttered imagery or low-contrast backgrounds.

Secondary Font
Inter is our workhorse typeface. It’s designed for digital readability and 
performs exceptionally well across UI, body copy, and system-generated 
content. While Satoshi speaks with boldness and vision, Inter brings balance 
and legibility to everyday communication.
Used thoughtfully, it supports hierarchy, enhances clarity, and ensures 
accessibility—without ever feeling mechanical.
Inter
Usage Guidelines
Use Inter for body text, UI elements, captions, and long-form content.
Stick to Regular or Medium weights for most use cases.
Pair with Satoshi to establish a clean and cohesive type system.
Maintain clear hierarchy: Inter supports our bold headlines while keeping the 
experience grounded and user-friendly.

Low-Fidelity Screens
We use low-fidelity representations of our product UI to communicate 
ideas, not pixels. These stripped-down screens are intentionally simplified 
to emphasize function over form—ideal for concept explanations, quick 
stakeholder alignment, and scalable brand thumbnails.
Low-fi mockups allow us to show core functionality without distracting 
users with fine details. They strike the right balance between design intent 
and visual clarity—making them perfect for storytelling across marketing, 
sales, product documentation, and training content.

High-Fidelity Screens
High-fidelity screens showcase the product exactly as users experience it
—visually rich, interactive, and true to functionality. These assets are used 
when precision and realism matter: in product marketing, documentation, 
training, and UI showcases.
These screens are fully styled, contain real data, and reflect the latest 
design system. They demonstrate both visual polish and functional depth, 
helping users and stakeholders engage with the product as it actually exists.

--- PAGE 29 ---
Principles
Photography and visual storytelling play a vital role in shaping how people 
perceive our brand. At Zluri, our visual language is built to feel modern, 
confident, and human—mirroring our belief that identity and access 
should empower people, not slow them down.
Our imagery captures real moments, real teams, and real impact—whether 
it’s IT teams in motion, decisions being made, or people navigating work 
with clarity. These visuals reflect our brand spirit: smart, approachable, 
and purposeful.
We show people working together—collaborating, 
deciding, and moving with purpose. Whether in person 
or remote, the focus is always on interaction and 
momentum.
Connected
Our visuals feel grounded in the real world—IT teams, 
digital tools, and everyday workspaces. Nothing overly 
staged, just clear, relatable moments.
Contextual
We capture people in control—solving, leading, and 
simplifying complexity. The tone is always bright, 
optimistic, and forward-moving.
Confident

--- PAGE 30 ---
Portraits
Our portrait photography should feel natural and human—capturing 
people in real environments with clarity and warmth. Faces are the focus, 
with clean backgrounds and soft depth of field that help subjects stand 
out without distraction.
We avoid overly posed or studio-style setups. Instead, we go for an 
editorial, lifestyle look: candid moments, natural light, and minimal styling. 
Images should feel authentic, approachable, and confident—reflecting the 
people we build for.

--- PAGE 31 ---
Testimonial Imagery
Our testimonial visuals reflect the industries, environments, and impact 
areas of our customers. These images aren’t about the people directly—
they’re about the context and scale of their work.
